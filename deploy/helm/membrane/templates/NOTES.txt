⚠️  SECURITY — ingestion webhook authentication:
  An empty credentials.webhookSecret DISABLES HMAC signature verification: the
  /webhook endpoint (port 8001) then accepts any unsigned POST into the pipeline.
  For any non-isolated cluster, set credentials.webhookSecret AND/OR restrict
  who can reach the ingestion Service (NetworkPolicy / mesh authz).
